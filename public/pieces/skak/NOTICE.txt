SKAK CHESS PIECE ASSET NOTICE

Files covered
-------------
bb.svg, bk.svg, bn.svg, bp.svg, bq.svg, br.svg,
wb.svg, wk.svg, wn.svg, wp.svg, wq.svg, wr.svg

Provenance
----------
These 12 SVG files are verbatim copies from Mu-Tsun Tsai's skak-svg repository
at commit aeef99c6b95cbe51990f402027349c982dc9b51f:
https://github.com/MuTsunTsai/skak-svg

That project states that it extracts chess assets from the LaTeX skak package,
normalizes the output to 100 px SVGs, flattens and minifies the objects, and
manually corrects some overflowing color masks. It credits the original set to
Piet Tutelaers and modifications to Torben Hoffmann.

The upstream skak work is available here:
https://github.com/lehoff/skak
https://ctan.org/pkg/skak

Changes in Knightfall
---------------------
No SVG geometry or colors were changed. The files were copied into
public/pieces/skak and are referenced by the React Piece component. This notice
and the upstream LPPL 1.3c text were added; the Knightfall source archive was
updated to include this asset directory.
